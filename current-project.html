PROJECT JOURNAL · SAMPLE EDITORIAL

A kitchen garden, one bed at a time.

Puurehof · Concept preview

This sample story imagines the first chapter of Puurehof. Alaina's actual project details will take its place before launch.

Begin with the kitchen

The plan starts with a simple question: what would we love to bring inside for dinner? A few herbs, tender greens, and tomatoes make a useful first sketch.

A little room to experiment

The concept pairs a small growing space with a place to pause: a pot of herbs near the door, a notebook for observations, and an open corner for something unexpected.

What this journal could share

Planting plans, honest progress photos, lessons from the first harvest, and recipes that follow the garden through the season.
